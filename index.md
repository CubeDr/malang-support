# Malang Support

Malang lets you turn your own photos into squishy characters and play with them on your device.

For help with the app, purchases, ads, or privacy, email [spaceship00@naver.com](mailto:spaceship00@naver.com). Please include your device model and app version (1.2.1) when reporting a problem. Do not send private photos unless you choose to.
