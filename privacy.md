# Malang Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-21

Malang is a playful image app that lets you create and interact with soft image objects from photos you choose.

## Information Malang Handles

Malang does not require an account, login, name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or payment information.

When you use the camera or photo library, Malang processes the image you select so it can create a Malang object. The app stores created Malang images and a small library manifest locally on your device in the app's private storage. These images are not uploaded to a Malang server by the app.

Malang also uses device motion sensor data to animate the app experience. This sensor data is used in real time on your device and is not stored by Malang.

## Advertising

Malang uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share data such as device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and advertising-related information for purposes including advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

For more information about how Google uses information from apps that use its services, see:

- https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Permissions

Malang may request access to:

- Camera: to let you take a photo and create a Malang object.
- Photos or media library: to let you choose an existing image and create a Malang object.
- Internet: to load and display ads.

## Data Sharing

Malang does not sell your personal information.

Malang does not share your selected photos with a Malang server. Advertising-related data may be collected or shared by Google AdMob as described above.

## Data Retention And Deletion

Images created in Malang are stored locally on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app.

To request help with privacy-related questions or deletion, contact: spaceship00@naver.com.

## Children

Malang is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us so we can help address the issue.

## Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be posted with a new "Last updated" date.
